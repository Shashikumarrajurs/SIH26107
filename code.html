Statutory Executive Summary

In India, consumer-grade stainless steel vacuum flasks and insulated drinkware fall under the statutory purview of the Ministry of Commerce & Industry Quality Control Orders (QCO). Domestic distribution mandates compliance with Indian Standard specifications ensuring non-toxic raw material grades, pressure durability, and leaching restrictions under Scheme-I certification.

Clause 4.2 — Material Quality Mandatory

"All components intended to come in direct contact with potable water shall be manufactured from austenitic stainless steel conforming to grade 304 (S30400) or superior, free from toxic lead and cadmium release."

Clause 6.1 — Thermal Retention & Sealing Testing Method

"When filled with water at 95°C ± 1°C and maintained at ambient 25°C, the internal liquid temperature after 6 hours shall not drop below 60°C. Zero hydraulic leakage at 0.5 bar inverted pressure."
